App title:
Number Merge Puzzle

Short description (under 80 characters):
Slide, merge and reach 2048 in this fun number puzzle!

Full description:
Number Merge Puzzle is a fun and simple number puzzle game. Slide the tiles on a 4x4 board and merge equal numbers. Two 2s make a 4. Two 4s make an 8. Keep merging and reach the 2048 tile. Can you do it?

The game is easy to learn and hard to stop. Every move brings a new tile. Every merge brings more points. Plan your moves, keep the board open, and chase your best score.

Features:
- Classic 2048-style merging on a 4x4 board
- Score and best score, saved on your device
- Move counter, so you can track every game
- One-step undo, to fix a wrong move
- New Game button, to start fresh at any time
- Win at 2048, with a clear game-over screen

Controls are simple. Swipe on the board, use the arrow keys, or tap the on-screen arrow buttons. The game works on phones and tablets.

Play offline, anywhere, at any time. No internet is needed to play. The game is free to play. Download Number Merge Puzzle now and see how far you can merge!
