# Privacy Policy for Number Merge Puzzle

**Last updated: 25 November 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what data Number Merge Puzzle ("the Game", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and what rights you have. Please read it carefully.

If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the Game.

## 1. What Data Is Collected

We collect only the data that is needed to run the Game and the leaderboard.

**When you create an account, we collect:**
- Your username.
- Your password, stored only as a bcrypt hash. We never store your plain password. We cannot see your password.
- The date and time your account was created.

**When you play and save a score, we collect:**
- Your game score.
- The date and time the score was saved.
- Your user id, so the score can be shown with your username on the leaderboard.

**On your own device, the Game stores:**
- Your best score, in your browser's localStorage. This stays on your device. It is not sent to our server unless you choose to save a score with your account.

## 2. What Is NOT Collected

We do **not** collect:
- No ads. The Game shows no advertisements.
- No ad tracking. We do not track you for advertising.
- No location data. We do not ask for or store your location.
- No contacts. We do not read your address book or contacts.
- No photos, no microphone, and no camera data.
- No device identifiers for advertising.
- No payment data. The Game is free. We do not ask for payment details.

## 3. How Data Is Used

We use the data only for these purposes:
- To let you create an account and log in.
- To save your game scores.
- To show the public leaderboard (top 20 scores). The leaderboard shows your username, your score, and the date of the score. It does not show your password or your email address.
- To keep the Game safe and working, for example to block abuse and fake scores.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers. We do not use your data for any other purpose.

## 4. How Data Is Stored and Protected

- Your account and score data are stored on our server in a SQLite database.
- Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Plain passwords are never stored.
- Login uses a JWT token. The token is signed with a secret that is kept on the server only.
- The server limits login attempts (rate limiting) to help stop brute-force attacks.
- Access to the server is limited to the people who run the Game.

No system is 100% safe. If a security problem happens, we will act to fix it and tell affected users if their data was involved.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with other companies, except:
- If the law requires it, we will share data when a lawful request is made.
- If the Game is run by a hosting provider, that provider may store the data on our behalf, under our instructions. The provider does not use the data for its own purposes.

## 6. User Rights

You have these rights over your data:
- **See your data:** you can ask us what data we hold about your account.
- **Correct your data:** you can ask us to fix data that is wrong.
- **Delete your account:** you can ask us to delete your account and your scores. Send a request to the contact email below with your username. We will delete your account and all scores linked to it. The leaderboard entries under your username will also be removed.
- **Stop using the Game:** you can stop using the Game at any time. If you do not want your best score saved on your device, clear your browser's localStorage or uninstall the app.

We will answer deletion and access requests within a reasonable time.

## 7. Children's Note

The Game is a simple number puzzle. It is suitable for general audiences, including children who play with a parent or guardian's permission.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without a parent or guardian's consent. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has created an account without your permission, contact us at the email below. We will delete the account and its data.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the Game adds a new feature. When we change it, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

If a change is important, we will also note it in the Game or on the store listing. Please check this page from time to time.
